# Brand voice

Edit this file (or send the bot `/voice <new text>`, or just tell it what to change) so captions sound like you.

## Who we are
A one-line description of the business or person posting.

## Tone
- Friendly and direct. Short sentences.
- Specific over generic: name the product, the place, the detail.

## Hashtags
- Instagram and TikTok: up to 3, relevant ones only.
- Nowhere else.

## Emojis
- At most one per post.

## Never
- "Game-changer", "elevate", "unlock", "in today's fast-paced world".
- Promises we can't keep (prices, dates) unless the user said them.

## Always
- End with a clear next step when there is one (visit, book, reply).
